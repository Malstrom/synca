# Competitive Landscape

This document maps the main competitors of Synca, grouped by proximity to our positioning.
It is the single source of truth for competitive analysis — all other docs reference this file.

> Last updated: 2026-06-01

---

## Synca Unique Positioning

No competitor currently combines all three pillars:

- **Real health data** (Apple Health, sleep, movement, daily rhythms — not just fitness)
- **Few, high-quality matches** (no infinite swipe by design)
- **Offline community + privacy/anti-fake**

---

## Cluster 1 — AI Matchmaking, No Swipe

Closest conceptual competitors: smart matching, intent-based dating, AI coach layer.

| App | Key Differentiator | Gap vs Synca |
|---|---|---|
| **SynchAI / synch.coach** | AI emotional coach, no swipe, compatibility on values & communication style | No health data; naming overlap risk ("synch" vs "synca") |
| **Sitch** | Chatbot matchmaker, paid per match, intent-driven | No health data |
| **Amata** | AI plans the first date, $25 commitment fee | No health data |
| **Known** | 20-min AI interview builds deep compatibility profile | No health/habit data |
| **Three Day Rule** | Human + AI hybrid curated matchmaking | Premium/manual, no health data |

### Naming Risk — SynchAI

SynchAI uses "Synch" and the claim *"Synch with who truly matters"*.
In English-language marketing, avoid using "synch" as a verb in Synca's main claims.
Prefer framing like: *"Synca — connect with people who live like you"* or *"your rhythms, your match"*.

## Cluster 2 — Health & Fitness Dating

Apps that use health or fitness signals for matching.

| App | Key Differentiator | Gap vs Synca |
|---|---|---|
| **Fitafy** | Fitness dating, integrates Apple Health & wearables for fitness milestones | Only gym/sport niche, not holistic health & lifestyle |
| **BandHealth** | Matches based on shared health conditions | Medical niche, not general wellness |

## Cluster 3 — AI Layer on Mainstream Apps

Existing large platforms adding AI matching — indirect competitors through mindshare.

| App | Key Differentiator | Gap vs Synca |
|---|---|---|
| **Hinge AI** | "Designed to be deleted", AI on profiles & suggestions | Swipe-based, no health data |
| **Tinder Chemistry** | Experimental AI matching | Swipe-based, volume-driven |
| **Bumble AI** | AI matchmaking announced | Swipe-based, no health data |

## Cluster 4 — AI Dating Tools (Indirect)

| Tool | Key Differentiator | Gap vs Synca |
