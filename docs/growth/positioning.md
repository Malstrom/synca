# Synca — Positioning
**Version:** 1.0
**Last updated:** May 2026
**Status:** Draft

---

## What Synca is

Synca is a lifestyle compatibility platform that uses passive behavioral data
(sleep, movement, music, travel) to find people whose daily life actually aligns
with yours — and surfaces a small number of high-quality connections, not an
infinite feed.

The entry mechanic is Spark: two people physically together scan a QR code and
receive an instant compatibility snapshot. No swiping. No algorithm needed to start.

---

## What Synca is not

- Not a swiping app. There is no feed to scroll.
- Not a self-reported matching app. What you write about yourself is not the signal.
- Not a social network. Connections are few and grounded in real-life encounter.
- Not a fertility or health tracker. Health data is used only for lifestyle alignment.

---

## The one-sentence pitch

> "Synca finds the people whose life actually fits yours — using the data you already
> collect every day, not what you choose to write about yourself."

## Positioning by audience

### For the user

**Problem they recognise:**
"I've been on three dates with someone great on paper and discovered after two months
that we can't sleep in the same room, have completely different energy levels, and
always want to do different things on weekends."

**What Synca says:**
"We use your Apple Health data to find people whose rhythms actually match yours.
Fewer connections. Much better ones."

**Tone:** honest, calm, slightly nerdy. No hype. No "find your soulmate" language.

---

### For the press

**Angle 1 — tech:**
"The first dating app that uses Apple Health data to predict compatibility before
the first date."

**Angle 2 — anti-Tinder:**
"While Tinder is optimised for engagement, Synca is optimised for outcomes.
It deliberately shows you fewer people."

**Angle 3 — Russia market:**
"Filling the vacuum left by Tinder and Bumble's exit from Russia with a
high-trust, privacy-first alternative."

**Story hook:**
The founding insight is concrete and relatable: most relationship friction is not
about values or interests — it is about sleep schedules, activity levels, and daily
rhythm. These are not things you can see on a profile. They show up after weeks
of spending time together. Synca detects them before.

---

### For the investor

**Market:** Dating apps global market $10B+. Russia specifically: Tinder + Bumble
exited, leaving a high-intent, underserved market of ~30M active dating app users.

**Differentiation:** Behavioral signal layer (Apple Health + HealthKit) creates a
moat that pure profile-based apps cannot replicate without a fundamental architecture
change. Data compounds over time — the longer a user is on Synca, the better the
signal.

**Monetisation:** Freemium. Algorithm-origin matches are premium. Spark (the primary
acquisition mechanic) is free for all users — every Spark is also a new user download.

**Traction hypothesis:** Spark virality means CAC is structurally low in Phase 0.
Every QR shared in a gym, run club, or sauna is an organic install.

Ref: `docs/investor/` for financial model and market analysis.

## Differentiators vs competitors

| Dimension | Tinder / Bumble | Hinge | Synca |
|---|---|---|---|
| Match signal | Photos + bio | Prompts + preferences | Passive behavioral data |
| Match volume | Infinite feed | Curated but still many | 1–3 per day max |
| Trust mechanism | None | Phone verify | TrustScore + Spark IRL |
| Acquisition mechanic | Ads + word of mouth | Ads + PR | Spark QR (organic, viral) |
| Health data | No | No | Core signal |
| IRL proof | No | No | Spark — strongest signal |
| Russia presence | Exited | Exited | Primary market |

## Language rules

**Use:**
- "lifestyle compatibility"
- "behavioral alignment"
- "your actual rhythm"
- "fewer, better connections"
- "passive data" / "data you already collect"
- "Spark" (always capitalised)

**Avoid:**
- "soulmate", "perfect match", "the one"
- "AI-powered" (we use behavioral signals, not a black-box AI)
- "dating app" as the primary frame (prefer "compatibility platform")
- Any medical or fertility language
- "algorithm" in user-facing copy (use "Synca suggested" instead)
